I am pleased to announce that I have been selected as a **Visiting Scholar at the Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST)** in Japan through the OIST Visiting Program.

My visit will take place from **25 March to 22 June 2027**, where I will pursue research in **Hybrid Quantum-Classical Systems** and engage with researchers across OIST's interdisciplinary research environment.

OIST's Visiting Program brings researchers from around the world to Okinawa to pursue independent research, interact with OIST research units, and contribute to the institute's interdisciplinary research community.

I am particularly looking forward to the opportunity to exchange ideas with researchers at OIST and to develop new collaborations during my stay.

[View my listing on the OIST Visiting Program website](https://www.oist.jp/visiting-program/visitors-list)
